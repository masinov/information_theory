# Round 2

## The outside critic on round 1 (fresh agent, no project history)

Main points, condensed:
- G1b, borne by the target, is stronger than D: bearing is realized, admitting is modal. Read as a
  scope statement, it chooses D rather than giving a reason for it.
- G2c follows from C and C-asym, but only if C-asym means "not even partly in virtue of". It also needs
  a ruling on chains (transitivity). And it answers reverse dependence, not the receiver worry: "R
  merely differing within itself" is already excluded by C's "partly in virtue of".
- G9 fails. D is existential ("F admits some differentiation"); E1-desc is universal (every registered
  difference). Case: D holds, but the registered articulation's δ belongs only to the description. What
  excludes this is C's "realized in F".
- G5/6 equivocates. P gives a difference between X and "what it is not"; D needs differentiation within
  F. Simple target case: X with no inner differentiation, distinct from Y. P's "what it is not" also
  reads as negation or alternative, against Reading A.
- Position case. If δ = "first position vs second position of the articulation", every articulation of
  every field yields a difference "borne by F". A test is needed that excludes merely positional
  respects.
- Self case. By C-asym, a field can register a contrast in itself only through a different contrast of
  itself. This should be stated.
- Missed formulations:
  - M1: D as a corollary of C (whatever satisfies C has a realized contrast in the target).
  - M2: P's dependence clause stated for contrasts: the distinguishing of X holds partly in virtue of a
    contrast in X.
  - M3: E1-cond with an antecedent about F, not about descriptions.
  - M4: a correctness condition on specification, excluding merely positional respects.
- Flag: "admits" is modal while alternatives are banned (this is Ps-modal, already open in the
  register; found here independently).
- Verdict: G1b as a gloss, not the ground; G2c kept at C; G9 dropped; G5/6 repaired through M1+M2,
  its best candidate.

## Assessment of the critique

Agreed:
- G9 fails for the reason given. Both exclusions come from C: E1-desc from "realized in F", the
  receiver worry from "partly in virtue of". C-asym adds a third: no reverse dependence.
- The draft agrees about the receiver worry: d6 places it in §3, where R and C are introduced, not in
  D's ground.
- The position case is the description worry in concrete form: a merely positional δ is a difference
  supplied by the notation. M4 is what the draft's guard (d1, d2) needs to be testable.
- C-asym's wording ("does not hold in virtue of") is ambiguous between "not even partly" and "not
  wholly". The derivation of G2c needs the first. This is a finding about an agreed entry, for the
  author.
- The self case follows from C-asym and should be stated.

Disagreed:
- "G1b chooses D rather than giving a reason." The draft says so itself: D is admitted as "a
  necessary condition for that route of explanation" (d2), not derived. That is a position, not a
  defect. The real question is a fork: D admitted (the draft) or D derived (M1).
- M1+M2 as the best candidate. M1 makes D a consequence of C, and that D is non-modal: "a registered
  target bears a realized contrast". The draft makes D modal on purpose: "Differentiability is
  consequently a modal commitment" (draft §1). M1 would reverse an explicit choice of the author's. It
  also dissolves Ps-modal by dropping "admits", which looks like a solution but is a change of subject.
  The cost must be stated.
- M2 restates C's second clause inside P, so P and C almost coincide. That restructures the whole core,
  beyond this exercise's scope. Kept as a fork, flagged.

Provenance note: P (the guiding premise) is not in the draft; it entered in Claude's rewrite and was
approved, restricted, by the author on 2026-10-24. The critic's point about "what it is not" therefore
bears on Claude's wording, approved by the author.

## a. Candidates

- **H1 (draft, with a test)**: D is admitted, as in the draft, as a condition of the route. The
  reconstruction concerns differences borne by the target. An articulation counts only if its contrast
  is realized in F in a respect that is not merely a feature of the notation, such as the bare
  difference between the positions the account assigns. The exclusions sit at C: description-only
  differences by "realized in F"; R merely differing within itself by "partly in virtue of"; reverse
  dependence by C-asym (read as "not even partly").
- **H1′ (the same, in the core's own relation)**: the guard stated with "in virtue of". The
  reconstruction concerns contrasts that do not hold wholly in virtue of their specification by the
  account. The receiver guard is then the parallel clause from C-asym: the target contrast does not
  hold even partly in virtue of the receiving contrast. Description and receiver guards are stated in
  one vocabulary, each at its own level.
- **H2 (M1, derived)**: D is not admitted but follows from C: whatever is registered bears a realized
  contrast. The core's premises are P and C. The description guard is M4, a condition on
  specification. The modal "admits" is replaced by "bears".
- **H3 (M1+M2)**: P's dependence clause, stated for contrasts, grounds C, and D follows. The core
  reduces to one premise and its consequences. Outside this exercise's scope; kept to record the
  direction.

## b. Critique

**H1**
- Fidelity: the closest to the draft (d1, d2, d6), with M4 as the test the draft lacked.
- Adversary: "a respect that is not merely a feature of the notation" needs a criterion. The bare
  difference between positions is the clear case. A respect defined only by the account's labels
  ("whatever the account calls the first term") is another. Is there a general mark?
- Proposed mark: a respect is merely notational if any two terms of any field would differ in it once
  articulated, so that its holding follows from the articulation alone.
- Consistency: compatible with P, C, Field-aspect, which applies it to R's articulations too, and
  Reading A.
- Translator: "We study only differences that hold in the target itself, not differences that hold
  just because we labelled two places differently."
- Survives.

**H1′**
- Adversary: it applies "in virtue of", the core's primitive dependence, to the relation between the
  account and the target. That is a statement about the theorist, not about what is described, which
  Ps-logic says the exposition must not mix up (d0).
- Reply: the sentence is about the account and says so. It does not attribute the account's activity
  to F. But a reader could take it for an instance of C, a registration by the theorist, which is G7
  again, dropped in round 1.
- Survives, with that risk noted.

**H2**
- Fidelity: it departs from the draft on two counts: D derived rather than admitted, and non-modal
  rather than modal.
- Gain: it removes the tension between "admits" and the ban on alternatives (Ps-modal), and the
  question of D's ground dissolves.
- Cost: D says nothing about an unregistered field. The draft's d3 ("F's admitting differentiation does
  not imply that anything differentiates it") loses its point. The order of exposition changes, since
  D can no longer come before C.
- Survives as the alternative to H1. The choice is the author's.

**H3**
- Scope: it restructures P and C. Not evaluated further here; recorded for the report.
