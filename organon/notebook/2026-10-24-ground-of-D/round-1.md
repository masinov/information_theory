# Round 1

## a. Candidates

Grounds of D (what D's ground guards against):

- **G1 (draft, d2)**: The reconstruction concerns differences attributable to its target, not supplied
  entirely by a description of it.
- **G2r (paper, p2; relata level)**: Not all differentiation that is registered is supplied by the
  receiver.
- **G2c (contrast level)**: A registered contrast does not hold in virtue of the receiving contrast
  that registers it.
- **G3 (both)**: Not supplied entirely by a description, nor by the receiver.
- **G4 (anything else)**: The differences concerned are attributable to the target, not supplied
  entirely by anything other than it.
- **G5 (from P)**: D is not a separate ground: by P, what is registered must be distinguishable in a
  way that depends on it, so a registered target bears differences of its own.
- **G6 (in the definition)**: Being a field includes bearing differences not supplied entirely by a
  description (Field-aspect); D then says the target is a field.
- **G7 (description as registration)**: A description of the target is one more registration of it,
  so description and receiver are one guard.
- **G8 (scope only)**: The reconstruction concerns only differences attributable to its target,
  asserting nothing about whether there are any.

Formulations of the exclusion (E1):

- **E1-desc**: Excluded: the differences registration concerns are borne only by descriptions of the
  target.
- **E1-recv**: Excluded: a registered contrast holds in virtue of the very receiving contrast that
  registers it.
- **E1-both**: both of the above, each with its own source.
- **E1-cond**: phrased as a conditional instead of the exclusion of a view: if all differences were
  supplied by descriptions, there would be no registration in P's sense.

## b. Critique

**G1**
- Fidelity: it is d2.
- Adversary: "attributable" is undefined. Any difference the account picks out is "attributable"
  if the account says so; the guard has no test.
- Counterexample: most articulations are mixed: the account chooses the respect δ, and the target
  bears the difference in that respect. G1 survives this through "entirely".
- Consistency: compatible with P and with Field-aspect, which extends it to the receiver.
- Translator: "We study only differences that belong to what we study, not differences our words put
  there."
- Survives, with "attributable" to be explicated.

**G2r**
- Fidelity: not in the draft as a ground. d6 is a different claim: that the receiver can have
  differences unrelated to the target.
- Consistency: it conflicts with the case Field-aspect leaves open, a field registering a contrast in
  itself. There the receiver is the target, so "not supplied by the receiver" becomes "not supplied
  by the target", which contradicts G1.
- Dropped at the level of relata.

**G2c**
- Fidelity: not in the draft. It is the contrast-level version of p2.
- Consistency: it follows from C and C-asym. C says the receiving contrast holds partly in virtue of
  the target contrast; C-asym says that the second then does not hold in virtue of the first. So
  G2c is a consequence, and cannot be the ground of D, which comes before C.
- Adversary: C-asym excludes the target contrast holding in virtue of the receiving contrast, but not
  in virtue of some other contrast of the receiver. A receiver could bring about a difference in the
  target through one of its contrasts and register it through another.
- Reply: that case is not a defect. Something brings about a difference in a target and registers
  it; the registration is still of a difference the target bears. What must be excluded is only
  registering, as the target's, a contrast that holds because of the very contrast that registers it.
  G2c excludes exactly that.
- Translator: "What is registered cannot be made by the very difference that registers it."
- Survives, as a consequence of C, not as a ground of D.

**G3**
- At the level of relata it inherits G2r's conflict. At the level of contrasts its receiver half is
  G2c, a consequence. As one ground it is redundant.
- Dropped as a single ground; kept in the form G9 below (each guard in its own place).

**G4**
- Adversary: it runs together two senses of "supplied": where a difference *lies* (which relatum
  bears it) and where it *comes from* (what brings it about). A target's difference can be brought
  about entirely by something else and still be borne by the target. The draft's contrast in d2 is
  with a description, which is not where the difference comes from but where it would be put
  instead of in the target. So "attributable" means borne, not originated.
- Dropped. The distinction between bearing and origin is kept: it is the explication G1 lacked.

**G5**
- Fidelity: the draft says D is "not deduced from the mere existence of F". P is not F's existence,
  so G5 does not contradict this, but it changes D's status from admitted to derived.
- Adversary: P's "distinguishable" has the same ambiguity as the argument for R (mec/F-subjectR):
  distinguishable by whom? If it is read as the account's distinguishing, P supports G1. If it is read
  as the receiver's, it brings back the subject.
- Consistency: G5 makes D conditional: if the target is registered, it bears differences. This
  agrees with the conditional reading of necessity (mec/Necessity, Claude's proposal, no authority).
- Structural consequence: with Field-aspect already making R a consequence, G5 would leave the core
  with P and C as its only premises. A large change; flagged, not assumed.
- Survives as a fork.

**G6**
- Adversary: D becomes true by definition ("the target is a field"), and the substance moves into
  the definition of field. That is not wrong if the core says plainly that it is a definition. But
  the claim that registered targets are fields is then G5 again.
- Survives as a fork about placement, joined with G5.

**G7**
- Consistency: under restricted P, aboutness by description is outside the core, so a description is
  not a registration in the core's sense. G7 would also make the theorist a receiver of the target,
  so that the account's own articulations become registrations, which is circular.
- Dropped.

**G8**
- This is G1 with its scope character made explicit: "concerns" in d2 already means this. The claim
  that such differences exist is made only conditionally, which is G5.
- Merged into G1.

**E1 variants**
- E1-desc follows from G1 and E1-recv from G2c, so E1-both places each where it comes from.
- E1-cond is a matter of phrasing, compatible with either.
- The label "pure constructivism" is not used in any of them.

## c. Revisions and forks

- **G1b** (G1 explicated by bearing): The reconstruction concerns differences borne by its target,
  whatever brings them about, not differences borne only by a description of it.
- **G9** (composite): G1b is the ground of D. G2c is a consequence of C and C-asym, stated where C is
  introduced. E1 splits into E1-desc (from D) and E1-recv (from C).
- **G5/6** (fork): D derived from P, conditional: whatever is registered bears differences of its own
  in the sense of G1b; "field" is defined accordingly (Field-aspect).

## Survivors sent to the outside critic
G1b, G2c, G9, G5/6, with E1-both and E1-cond.

## Dropped this round
- G2r: conflicts with Field-aspect's open case of self-registration.
- G3: redundant once each half is placed.
- G4: confuses bearing with origin.
- G7: description is not registration under restricted P; circular.
- G8: merged into G1.
