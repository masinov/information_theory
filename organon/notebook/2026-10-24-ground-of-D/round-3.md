# Round 3

## The outside critic on round 2 (fresh agent)

Main points, condensed:
- H1's route-of-explanation ground supports only an existential, non-modal claim (the articulated
  targets bear contrasts); the modal "admits" is unearned. In H1, D does no excluding work at all.
- H1′'s "not wholly in virtue of its specification" concedes partial creation by the account,
  contradicting "selects, does not create", and lets through mixed respects (genuine AND first
  position). The two guards become uneven (not wholly / not even partly).
- H2 is a weakening, not a derivation: it drops a conjunct of C. As a lemma for registered cases it
  even follows from P alone.
- The universality mark for "merely notational" misclassifies:
  - Notational but passes: "a is specified with more symbols than b"; conjunctions with a positional
    conjunct.
  - Genuine but fails: a respect in which every field really contrasts.
- Better mark: invariance under re-specification. δ is merely notational if whether, or which way,
  a and b differ in δ changes when the same positions of F are relabelled, reordered or redescribed,
  with F unchanged. Plus a separate non-triviality condition: δ is not satisfied by every pair of
  distinct positions. "The same positions" can be fixed only by F's own differentiation, which is
  what D supplies; this gives D a job.
- D can stay modal without alternatives if the modality ranges over specifications, not over states
  of F: F bears at least one contrast between actual, co-present positions, so that an articulation,
  if specified, would be true of F. "No particular differentiation" must then mean "D does not say
  which" (reading a), not "F need not bear any" (reading b), which needs non-actual states of F.
- H1′'s feared risk (theorist as receiver) is not real, since the direction is C-asym's, not C's. And
  an accurate account would anyway satisfy C (by Field-aspect the account is a field), so the
  description guard comes free from C-asym for accurate accounts.
- Missed: "realized in F" needs "in respect δ"; guard (ii) should concern the receiving contrast only
  (R may register its own effects through another contrast); D with Field-aspect is near-definitional
  for unregistered fields; P's "depend on X" should be dependence on a contrast involving X's position.
- Verdict: H1, with three repairs: the modal reading over specifications; the invariance test; "not
  even partly" for both guards, merged into one clause of C-asym's shape.

## Assessment

Agreed:
- The invariance test replaces the universality mark. Its need for "same positions" anchored in F is a
  gain: it is the clearest job D has.
- "Not even partly in virtue of its specification." This is the draft's d1 and the paper's p3
  ("selects, does not create") stated exactly.
- H2 reduces to a lemma.
- Guard (ii) concerns the receiving contrast only. This matches round 1's reply on production by
  another contrast.

Already covered, which the critic could not see: "realized in respect δ" is in Df-realized: "F
exhibits it: its terms are both actual and differ in δ".

Disagreed: "the description guard comes free from C-asym, since the theorist is a receiver." Under
restricted P (Dec-restrictP, agreed), the core covers basic aboutness, prior to concepts and
descriptions. A theory is descriptive aboutness, so the account is not a receiver in the core's
sense. The description guard cannot be inherited from C; it must stay where the draft puts it, as a
condition of the route (d2). The parallel in shape between the two guards remains, as a parallel,
not a derivation.

New fork, found by setting the critic's modal reading against the draft:
- The draft's §2: "From the claim that F admits differentiation, it does not follow that a
  differentiation has been realized." If "realized" means that F bears a contrast, the draft holds
  reading (b): F need not bear any. If it means that a specified articulation holds, the draft is
  compatible with reading (a).
- The draft was written before Reading A. Reading (b) without alternatives needs "admits" to be a real
  capacity of F to bear contrasts it does not bear. That is the capacities branch (mec/B-power), with
  its cost: capacities identified by what they would do (E4 risk).
- Reading (a) needs no new ontology, and fits d1, "a capacity to participate in a differentiated
  articulation", read as: to be truly articulated.
- This is the author's decision.

Open question (the critic's, endorsed): does D concern every field, or only the targets of the
reconstruction? The draft's d2 says the reconstruction "concerns" its targets, which suggests the
second. Under the conditional reading of necessity, the core needs nothing about unregistered fields.

## a. Candidates

**K1a (H1 repaired, reading a)**
- D: the targets of the reconstruction bear contrasts between actual, co-present positions, in
  respects not yet specified; D does not say which. "Admits" ranges over specifications: any
  articulation that is specified selects one of these contrasts or fails.
- Ground of D (route condition, d2): the reconstruction concerns contrasts that do not hold even
  partly in virtue of their specification by the account.
  - Test: invariance under re-specification, plus non-triviality.
  - Anchor for "same positions": the contrasts the target bears, as D says.
- At C:
  - "realized in F", in respect δ (Df-realized): excludes description-only contrasts in registered
    cases;
  - "partly in virtue of": excludes R merely differing within itself;
  - C-asym, read as "not even partly": excludes a registered contrast holding in virtue of the
    receiving contrast that registers it.
- E1 split:
  - E1-desc: excluded, the contrasts registration concerns hold, even partly, in virtue of their
    specification (from D's ground, and "realized in F");
  - E1-recv: excluded, a registered contrast holds, even partly, in virtue of the receiving contrast
    (from C and C-asym);
  - and R merely differing within itself, excluded by C.
  - Optional conditional phrasing (round 1's M3): if the target bore no contrast, no articulation of
    it would be realized, C could not hold, and nothing in it would be registered.
- Wording consequence: the paper's "no particular differentiation is thereby realized or specified"
  would read "D specifies no articulation, and does not say which contrasts the target bears".

**K1b (H1 repaired, reading b)**
- D: the targets of the reconstruction have a capacity to bear contrasts, whether or not they bear
  any. The capacity is F's own (Ps-modal), not a stock of possibilities.
- Ground, guard, test and E1 split as in K1a; the invariance test's anchor becomes the contrasts the
  target actually bears when it is articulated.
- Cost: imports the capacities branch into D, and with it the question whether a capacity can be
  identified without what it would do (E4).

## b. Critique

**K1a**
- Fidelity: it keeps D admitted, modal (over specifications) and before C, as in the draft. It reads
  the draft's §2 "realized" as "specified articulation holding", which is the paper's sense, not
  necessarily the draft's.
- Adversary: is the invariance test epistemically circular? To apply it one must know which positions
  F bears, which is what one is testing. Reply: the test says what "merely notational" means, not how
  to find out. That is acceptable for a definition, but the report should say so.
- Adversary: restricting D to the targets of the reconstruction makes D relative to the account's
  choice of targets. Reply: only its scope is relative; what D says of a target does not depend on the
  account.
- Translator: "Whatever we take as a target already differs within itself in ways we have not yet
  named; naming picks out one of these differences and does not make it."
- Survives.

**K1b**
- Fidelity: possibly the draft's §2 reading.
- Adversary: it makes the core's first commitment depend on a contested ontology of capacities. By
  M2 (keep what is not forced open as a branch), it should be a branch, not D.
- Survives as the alternative for the author's decision, with its cost stated.
