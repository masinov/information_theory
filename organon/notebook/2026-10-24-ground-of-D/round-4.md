# Round 4

## The outside critic on round 3 (fresh agent)

Main points, condensed:
- K1a's "admits" is idle. "Any specified articulation selects a contrast or fails" holds of every
  field. D's content is non-modal (the targets bear contrasts); the only modality left ranges over the
  account's possible specifications. Claimed: this reverses the draft's intent, moving the gap between
  "admitted" and "realized" to the account's side.
- The invariance test is circular. Positions are individuated by the contrasts under test; anchoring by
  other contrasts regresses. It also compares the actual description with alternative ones.
- E1-desc is badly sourced. The ground relates the account to its targets, which is aboutness by
  description, outside the core's scope under restricted P. So it is a constraint on the account, and
  E1-desc is a scope exclusion, not a result of the core. "In virtue of" between a contrast and a
  specification is not established as C's relation.
- Receiver gap: nothing excludes a receiving contrast supplied by description, unless D's ground is
  applied to R as well.
- K1b: a capacity cannot be identified without what it would do. Every candidate either collapses into
  K1a or moves the modality into Field-aspect's "subject to". A primitive capacity does no work.
- Temporal and order wording: "not yet specified", "F unchanged", "co-present" undefined; "which way"
  gives contrasts a direction.
- Transitivity: C-asym is two-place, so a loop c ← r2 ← r1 ← c escapes E1-recv unless "partly in
  virtue of" is transitive. Transitivity makes it a strict partial order among contrasts.
- P relates to X, a relatum, while C relates contrasts: a bridge is needed ("depends on X" = "holds in
  virtue of contrasts in which X is a term").
- Gerrymandered respects (Boolean combinations of genuine ones) pass every test. This may be acceptable
  for necessary conditions, but it should be said.
- Verdict: neither as stated. K1a as the base, with D non-modal; the ground as a definition ("merely
  notational iff the contrast holds even partly in virtue of its specification"); the test as a
  heuristic; the ground placed outside the core; D and its ground extended to R; transitivity stated;
  wording fixed.

## Assessment

Agreed:
- "Admits" in K1a adds nothing beyond "the targets bear some contrast".
- The invariance test is circular as a definition. It is kept as a heuristic.
- The ground is a condition on the method: the account's relation to its targets is descriptive
  aboutness, outside the core's scope (as already argued in round 3). E1-desc is a scope exclusion.
- The receiver gap is already closed by Field-aspect, agreed with the author, which applies the
  draft's d2 to any field, the receiver included. It must be said explicitly in the formulation.
- Wording: "unspecified", not "not yet specified"; "the same F", not "F unchanged"; drop "which way"
  from the test.
- Gerrymandered respects: state the permissiveness. The core gives necessary conditions only.

Disagreed, from the draft:
- "Reading admits over specification reverses the draft's intent." The draft's own statement of D is:
  "F admits differentiation, without a particular differentiation having been selected" (draft §1).
  And §2: "differentiability permits articulation, but neither produces an actual articulation nor
  determines its form. An account of a particular case must therefore supply the articulation it
  concerns." In the draft the gap is between admitted and selected, and selection is the account's
  act. Read this way, the draft's "realized" (§2, "it does not follow that a differentiation has been
  realized") means a selected articulation holding, as in the paper. The specification reading is
  close to the draft's words, not against them.
- What remains open is whether "admits" should mean more than "some articulation of it can be
  specified truly". If more, only a capacity of F is left, and all three critics found that a capacity
  cannot be stated without what it would do. This is the author's decision.

Not adopted: "merely notational iff the contrast holds even partly in virtue of its specification". It
applies C's primitive ("in virtue of") to the relation between account and target, which the core
does not cover (the critic's own point). The draft and the paper already have plain words for it:
specification "selects", it does not "create".

Beyond this exercise, for the author:
- Transitivity of "partly in virtue of". If accepted, indirect loops are excluded and C generates an
  order among contrasts: a consequence of registration, not a precondition, consistent with E5 and
  with the process frame (mec.process/Order). If not, E1-recv excludes only direct reverse dependence.
- P's wording, found by two independent critics: "distinguishable from what it is not" reads as a
  negation or an alternative under Reading A; and "depend on X" relates to a relatum where C relates
  contrasts. P is Claude's wording, approved by the author in restricted form.
- "Co-present" (Reading A, Df-conditions) has no stated meaning that is not temporal or spatial.

## a. Candidate

**L1**
- D: the targets of the reconstruction bear contrasts between actual positions, whether or not any is
  specified. To say a target admits differentiation is to say no more than this: some articulation of
  it can be truly specified. D does not say which.
- Ground of D, a condition of the route, at the level of method: the reconstruction concerns contrasts
  its targets bear. Specification selects such contrasts; it does not create them. A respect is merely
  notational when its contrast exists only through being specified; such respects are outside the
  reconstruction. Heuristic for applying this: a merely notational respect changes its verdict when the
  same F is articulated with other labels or in another order.
- Scope: this holds for every field the account articulates, the receiver included (Field-aspect).
- Registration (C), for registered cases:
  - "realized in F, in respect δ" (Df-realized);
  - "partly in virtue of": R does not merely differ within itself;
  - C-asym, read as "not even partly": a registered contrast does not hold even partly in virtue of the
    receiving contrast that registers it. Indirect loops depend on transitivity, a decision left to the
    author.
- E1 becomes three exclusions with separate sources:
  - E1-desc, a scope exclusion from the route condition: contrasts that exist only through being
    specified are not what the reconstruction is about;
  - E1-self, from C: R merely differing within itself is not registration;
  - E1-recv, from C and C-asym: a registered contrast does not hold even partly in virtue of the
    receiving contrast.
- Alternative kept for the author: a modal D beyond articulability is possible only as a capacity of
  F, which belongs in the capacities branch (mec/B-power), not in D.

## b. Critique

- Fidelity: D's statement keeps the draft's "admits … without a particular differentiation having been
  selected". The draft calls D "a modal commitment"; L1 gives that modality a precise and modest
  content (articulability). The draft's ground (d2) becomes explicitly a condition on the method, as
  "necessary condition for that route of explanation" already says.
- Adversary: is "some articulation of it can be truly specified" itself an alternative ("can")? Reply:
  the "can" ranges over acts of the account, outside the core, not over states of F. The ban on
  alternatives (Reading A) concerns the terms of contrasts, and no contrast in F gets a non-actual term.
  This reading of the ban's scope should be stated.
- Adversary: with the ground at the level of method, does D still belong to the core? Reply: D's
  content (the targets bear contrasts) is a claim about the targets. Only its reason for being admitted
  is methodological. Under rule M4, results that rest on D rest partly on method; this should be
  recorded.
- Consistency: P (scope), Field-aspect (extension to R), Reading A (narrowed as above), E5, E6 (no
  subject in F; the account appears only in the method).
- Translator: "What we study already differs within itself; when we name a difference we pick it out,
  we do not make it; and differences that exist only because we named them are not our subject."
- Survives.
