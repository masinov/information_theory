# Round 6 (last)

## The outside critic on round 5 (fresh agent)

Fatal as worded, each repairable:
- F1: Defining a term as "a position in a contrast the field bears" makes every articulation realized,
  so E1-desc names something impossible and D follows from the definition (circular). Repair: two
  levels. Specified terms (what a description picks out, which may pick out nothing) and positions
  (places in a contrast a field bears). Realized iff the specified terms pick out positions of a
  contrast the field bears in δ. "Bears a contrast in δ" is taken as primitive.
- F2: "For registered targets D follows from P and C" needs three bridges:
  - (a) registration requires that some articulation be realized and matched;
  - (b) P is about X, a term, while D is about F, the field bearing the contrast in which X is a term;
  - (c) "exhibits" read as "bears".
  With these, D-core is a theorem about the bearing field, not about every field.
- F3: Field-aspect's "subject to differentiation" has three readings: modal (then the modal D is
  already in the core), "bears a contrast", or "is differentiated by some description" (which defeats
  D). L2 depends on the reading and does not choose.

Repairable:
- R1: status mix (a core claim resting on method). Split into D-core (a theorem) and D-scope (a
  restriction of method).
- R2: the working condition is about articulations (the specified contrast is borne by the target);
  D's existential claim adds nothing for unregistered targets.
- R3: the specifiability assumption is modal and ungrounded. Replace it by scope: the reconstruction
  concerns only contrasts the account specifies.
- R4: "does not create" is stipulated, not derived; say so.
- R5: E1-desc is coherent, since notation is a field by Field-aspect. But:
  - add "where the bearer is not the target";
  - mark order and labels as examples;
  - cover relational respects (target positions related to notation positions), not only
    conjunctions: any respect whose holding depends partly on the specification.
- R6: the heuristic is silent on asymmetric δ. Require invariance under (a,δ,b) → (b,δ⁻¹,a), with δ's
  direction stated in terms of the respect, not the slot.
- R7: ask the author whether "partly in virtue of" is transitive. Note that C-asym implies
  irreflexivity, so E1-self's "distinct contrast" is derived.

Faithfulness, unflagged departures: the draft's "without a particular differentiation having been
selected" may mean F is not yet differentiated in any particular way, while L2 requires F to bear a
particular unnamed contrast; likewise "neither … determines its form"; partial supply by a related
description, which the draft may allow and L2 excludes; D derived rather than admitted.

The dilemma for the modal D: stated without a reservoir, "F admits differentiation" means "some
articulation of F, if specified, would be realized". Realization needs both terms actual. So the modal
D is either L2's D plus specifiability (an actual contrast grounds it) or it needs non-actual terms,
which the core forbids.

## Assessment

Accepted: F1, F3 (as a question for the author), R1–R7, and the dilemma, which is a better argument
than the earlier "a capacity cannot be stated without what it would do".

F2 is partly answered by the core's own definitions, which the critic did not have. Df-registration:
"A specified articulation realized in F is registered by R when C holds between them." So registration
always is of a realized articulation: bridge (a) is in the definition. Df-realized: "F exhibits it: its
terms are both actual and differ in δ"; with F1's two levels, "exhibits" is "bears" (c). So D-core
follows from Df-registration and Df-realized alone; P is not needed for it. Bridge (b) is a scope
point: D-core concerns the field that bears the registered contrast, not each term of it.

Consequence worth stating to the author: with R1, D is no longer an admitted premise of the core. It
splits into a theorem (D-core) and a rule of method (D-scope). The draft's "Differentiability is
admitted" would change. That is the largest structural result of the exercise, and a conservative
alternative keeps D admitted.

On Field-aspect (F3): the author's own words were "insofar as something is distinguished/subject of
differentiation, it is Field", together with the sentence from the draft added at the author's request
(differences "attributable to its target, rather than one supplied entirely by an unrelated
description"). That sentence excludes the third reading. Between the first (modal) and the second
(bears a contrast) the author must choose; the dilemma bears on the choice.

On partial supply: a respect whose holding depends partly on the specification is not borne by the
target alone. If the reconstruction concerns what the target bears, such respects are outside it.
This is stronger than the draft may intend; it is presented as a departure.

## a. Final formulation

**L3**

Levels.
- A specified term is what a description picks out; it may pick out nothing.
- A position is a place in a contrast a field bears.
- "Field F bears a contrast in respect δ" is primitive.
- An articulation ⟨a,δ,b⟩ of F is realized iff a and b pick out positions of a contrast F bears in δ.
  The direction of δ, if any, is stated in terms of the respect, not by the order of the slots.

D-core (theorem): every field in which a registered articulation is realized bears that articulation's
contrast. From Df-registration and Df-realized.

D-scope (method): the reconstruction articulates only fields taken to bear the contrasts it specifies,
and concerns only contrasts the account specifies. Specification chooses the respect and selects a
contrast borne in it; that it does not create the contrast is a stipulation of the route, as the draft's
"admitted … a necessary condition for that route of explanation" says.

What the route does not do: argue against the view that all differentiation is supplied. It states that
view's cost: if it held, nothing would be registered in P's sense.

Heuristic (method only): relabelling the terms, or replacing ⟨a,δ,b⟩ by ⟨b,δ⁻¹,a⟩, where δ⁻¹ is the
converse respect, must not change whether the articulation is realized.

Exclusions, each with its source.
- E1-desc (scope, from D-scope): a respect whose holding depends, even partly, on the specification or
  its notation (order, labels and grouping of terms are examples), where the bearer of that respect is
  not the target. This includes relational respects that relate target positions to notation
  positions.
- E1-self (from C): a receiver differing within itself, where its contrast does not hold even partly in
  virtue of the target's contrast, is not registration. A field may register one of its own contrasts,
  but only through a distinct contrast. That the contrast is distinct follows from C-asym, which implies
  that nothing holds in virtue of itself.
- E1-recv (from C and C-asym): a registered contrast does not hold, even partly, in virtue of the
  receiving contrast that registers it. Whether this extends to chains depends on whether "partly in
  virtue of" is transitive (the author's decision).

## b. Final internal check

- Alternatives: none among the terms of contrasts. The heuristic's relabellings are operations on the
  description, at the level of method.
- Subject: none in any field. The account appears only in D-scope and the heuristic.
- Order: only the direction of δ, stated in terms of the respect. Among contrasts, order arises only if
  transitivity is accepted, and then as a consequence of C, consistent with E5.
- Circularity: none. "Bears a contrast" is primitive. D-core follows from the definitions of
  registration and realization, and D-scope is method.
- Translator: "Whatever we describe as registered differs within itself; when we describe a difference
  we pick it out, and if it holds only through our describing, it is not our subject."

Stopping: round 5 and round 6 each revised the same formulation (L1 → L2 → L3) and produced no new
surviving formulation; this is also the sixth round.
