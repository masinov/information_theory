# Round 5

## The outside critic on round 4 (fresh agent)

Main points, condensed:
- Faithfulness:
  - Direct conflict with the draft's "a modal commitment: it concerns what F admits". L1 keeps
    modality only over the account's acts.
  - Tension with "neither … determines its form": in L1 F fixes the menu of contrasts.
  - Tension with "positions, not prior objects": positions that exist before any articulation.
  - Minor loss: "unrelated" and "entirely" dropped.
- E1-desc as stated is empty: if specification only selects, nothing "exists only through being
  specified". Repair: contrasts borne by the specification or its notation (order, labels) and wrongly
  attributed to the target.
- The heuristic presupposes F identified apart from articulation, wrongly flags directional respects
  (swapping terms of an asymmetric δ), and misses notational respects that are invariant. Restrict it
  to relabelling, and to reordering only for symmetric δ; mark it as account-level.
- Ground: for registered targets, D follows from P and C inside the core; the method-level ground is
  needed only for articulated fields never registered. Cost to state: against the view that all
  differentiation is supplied, L1 does not argue, it restricts scope.
- The ban on alternatives limited to terms is legitimate (the ban already speaks of terms). But "can be
  truly specified" brings possible specifications into D's wording. And the two statements of D are
  not equivalent without an assumption that the account can express every borne contrast.
- Reintroductions: "verdict" (a judge) in the heuristic; "matched" in C undefined (a counterfactual
  reading brings back alternatives, a pairing by the theorist brings back a knower); order in the
  heuristic and in the direction of dependence.
- Missed:
  - with C-asym pairwise and no transitivity, a cycle A→B→C→A evades E1-recv; fix by "no cycles along
    chains", without asserting transitivity;
  - "partly" is very weak, so the constraint rests on "matched";
  - a field with one position bears no contrast, so a lone X with no actual other term cannot be
    registered: a substantive commitment to state;
  - partial supply (mixed respects) falls between E1-desc and admission.
- Verdict: accept with ten named repairs; without 1 (D non-modal on F's side), 4 (E1-desc restated) and
  7 (no cycles), L1 fails.

## Assessment

Accepted: repairs 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6, and 10 (E1-self clarified; mixed respects). Stated as the author's
decision: repair 2 (the draft's "modal" sentence). Flagged beyond this exercise: 7, 8, 9.

On the three points of faithfulness:
- "Modal commitment": real. L2 below states D without modality. Keeping the draft's sentence requires
  a capacity (the capacities branch), with the cost three critics found. The choice is the author's,
  and the report must say so plainly.
- "Neither … determines its form": in the draft, the form at issue is "whether its terms would be
  objects, qualities, regions, or something else" (draft §1). D, the commitment, still determines none
  of that; F may. Read so, there is no conflict. Weaker than the critic says, but worth noting.
- Positions: the draft says the notation "distinguishes positions within the articulation being
  described" and assumes no prior objects. L2 reconciles the two: a term is a position in a contrast
  the field bears; an articulation's terms are those positions as the description picks them out. No
  term is a prior object, and none is a slot created by describing.

On "entirely": the draft's "supplied entirely by an unrelated description" allows the account to
contribute the choice of respect (it does, by specifying) but not the difference itself. Under the
bearing reading: the account chooses δ; the difference in δ is borne by the target or not. A mixed
respect (one borne by the target conjoined with one borne by the notation) is, as a whole, not borne by
the target, and falls under E1-desc; its target-borne part can be specified on its own.

Agreed with emphasis: the core does not refute the view that all differentiation is supplied. It shows
what that view would cost: if it held, nothing would be registered in P's sense. This is the author's
own correction of Claude's "anti-constructivism", arrived at again from another direction.

## a. Candidate

**L2**
1. D (in the core, on the side of the field, non-modal):
   - Every field the reconstruction articulates bears at least one contrast between actual terms of
     its own. D does not say which, nor of what form.
   - A term is a position in a contrast the field bears; an articulation's terms are those positions as
     a description picks them out.
   - For registered targets, D follows from P and C. For targets not registered, it rests on the route
     condition.
2. Route condition (method, not a claim of the core):
   - The reconstruction concerns contrasts its targets bear. Specification chooses the respect and
     selects a contrast borne in it; it does not create the contrast.
   - Assumed in applying this: any contrast relevant to the reconstruction can be specified by the
     account.
   - The route does not argue against the view that all differentiation is supplied. It shows that view's
     cost: if it held, nothing would be registered in P's sense.
3. Heuristic (method only): relabelling the terms, or reordering them where δ is symmetric, must not
   change whether the articulation is true of the target.
4. Exclusions, each with its source:
   - E1-desc (scope, from the route condition): contrasts borne by the specification or its notation,
     such as the order or labels of terms, and attributed to the target. A mixed respect falls under
     E1-desc as a whole; its target-borne part may be specified on its own.
   - E1-self (from C): a receiver differing within itself, where its contrast does not hold even partly
     in virtue of the target's contrast, is not registration. Not excluded: a field registering one of
     its own contrasts through a distinct contrast of its own.
   - E1-recv (from C and C-asym): a registered contrast does not hold, even partly, in virtue of the
     receiving contrast that registers it. Indirect dependence through a chain is excluded only if
     C-asym is extended to chains (no cycles), which is the author's decision.
5. Alternative for the author: a modal D, as in the draft's "a modal commitment: it concerns what F
   admits", is available only as a capacity of the field (the capacities branch), with its cost.
   Otherwise the draft's sentence is revised to state D's content as above.

## b. Critique

- Fidelity: it departs from the draft in one place, the modal sentence, and says so. It keeps "without
  a particular differentiation having been selected", "permits articulation, but neither produces an
  actual articulation nor determines its form", "the account must supply the articulation", and the
  ground, now placed as method.
- Adversary: "for registered targets, D follows from P and C" makes D redundant there. What does D do?
  Reply: it states what the core requires of a target before any receiver enters; for unregistered
  targets it is the only statement of this. The redundancy for registered cases is a result, not a
  defect.
- Adversary: the expressibility assumption (anything relevant can be specified) is strong. Reply: it
  is an assumption of the method, not of the core. Contrasts the account cannot express are simply not
  reconstructed; the core says nothing false about them.
- Check for reintroductions: no alternative term in any contrast; no subject in F (the account appears
  only in the method); order only in the heuristic, as reordering of terms in the description, not
  among contrasts in F.
- Translator: "Whatever we take as a target already differs within itself between actual terms. When
  we describe a difference we choose the respect and pick the difference out; we do not make it.
  Differences that belong only to our description are not our subject."
- Survives.

## Flagged beyond this exercise (for the author)
- C-asym extended to chains (no cycles), so that E1-recv also covers indirect dependence. Not a claim of
  transitivity.
- "Matched" in C needs a definition that is neither counterfactual nor fixed by the theorist; see also
  mec/F-pairing.
- P's wording, raised by three independent critics: "distinguishable from what it is not" should read
  "contrasts with some actual other term"; "depend on X" should be "hold partly in virtue of contrasts
  in which X is a term".
- "Co-present" (Reading A, Df-conditions) needs a meaning that is not temporal or spatial, or should
  be dropped as redundant with "actual".
- A lone term with no actual other term cannot be registered: a consequence of P and Reading A, to be
  stated.
- "Partly" is weak; most of C's constraint rests on "matched".
