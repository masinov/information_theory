## Section by section

**Abstract.** Rewritten. States that the core is not itself an epistemic relation; two commitments and
one decision; no "it follows" for what is assumed. (A1–A12)

**§1 Introduction.** Scope narrowed to what involves something's being taken up (I2, I3). "Taken up"
said to be unanalysed, "registration" reserved, the claim and its false converse stated (A1, P1).
"Linguistic" removed; the sentence beginning "For the same reason" removed; the sentence on
conventions removed, and the one convention left (the letters F and R) is marked where it occurs
(I5). "Reconstruction" glossed (I6). The list of kinds matches the table (I7).

**§2 Two Commitments and a Decision.** New. (D) with its two parts, its reason marked as a reason and
not a proof, what it does not say, and two ways of refusing it (P4–P8, P11). (C) as what says which
relation the account is about (P9, P10); the relations it sets aside are treated once, in §5 (C7).
The decision, with three reasons (P15–P18) and its cost (P19). What kind of claim (P20, P21).

**§3 Fields and Contrasts.** Three conditions and two remarks (F3). Fields not individuated, said in
the text (F3, K1). Identity of contrasts by terms and respect (Ar4). Scope condition reworded; called
a condition of method that agrees with (C) (F7). "Either" removed (F8).

**§4 Articulation.** An articulation names two terms and a respect; realized when the terms named are
actual terms of F and differ in that respect; two ways of failing (Ar1–Ar4). The author's neutral
sentence on prior objects kept, with "and it does not deny it"; the footnote no longer attributes a
refusal to the account (Ar2, Ar5). "δ is the respect in which the two differ" (Ar6). δ*: left out
because it holds partly through how the account has written the terms, "and not because something
other than F has a part in it" (Ar8). The former (D) is stated without a label (Ar9, P-summary).
"The first limit" removed (Ar10).

**§5 Receptive Dependence.** Three conditions (C5). The conditions are said to be stated for two
contrasts, and (C) to relate a taking-up to what is taken up (P14, C2). Permitted or open: partial,
mutual, transitive, with the connection between the last two (C8). Three relations that can hold
without dependence, with the figure relabelled so that it uses no letters introduced later (C7).
"Systematic or relevant coupling" kept as a standalone point without "for the same reason" (C9).
"Need not be fixed by a description" (C10). Registration does not single out one contrast; which a
taking-up is of is not settled (C11). Roles defined on fields through the contrasts they bear (C12).

**§6 The Receiving Field.** New first three paragraphs: what the account takes the taking-up to be, by
the decision, with two assumptions; "the least secure step of the account, and it is marked as such"
(P14, R4). (R) as before, with its two grounds (R2). The author's point that R distinguishes nothing
(R3). "Form" removed (R6). "The second consequence" removed (R1).

**§7 Registration.** One definition (G1). The three steps from the commitments to the structure, with
their kinds, and the converse denied (P-summary, C2). "Does not require that anything pass" (G4).
"Which way a term is", not "F is a rather than b" (G6). The repertoire sentence no longer joins two
arguments (G7).

**§8 Two Cases.** Causal case: the paper says that something does pass and that a process in time is
involved, and that the core neither requires nor excludes either (E2). Second case in letters, with
three stated differences from the first, one of which is that the dependence is fixed by description
(E3, C10). Third result: registration is not itself epistemic (E7).

**§9 Registration and Order.** "It does not say that the conditions of R are successive, and it does
not say that they are not" (O1). "Appears to require" throughout (O3). A sentence on the dependence
as a possible order, if transitive (C8).

**§10 Conclusion.** Table rebuilt: ten rows, including the two unargued assumptions and what is
presupposed (K1). "Contains", not "is" (K2). One thing ruled out, one left out as method, five not
included (K3). Eight things left undone, among them what must be added to a registration (K5). Five
questions; the third is new (mec/Q6); "which way F is" removed (K6).

## Found on reading the new text before installing it
- The first reason for the decision, as first drafted, undermined itself: it said that differing from
  a way nothing is "is nothing more than its being as it is", from which it would follow that holding
  in virtue of the one is holding in virtue of the other. Replaced by the author's own reason: such a
  way has to be supplied from somewhere, and the three sources are a stock of possibilities, a
  repertoire, or the account's specification.
- "Registered" and "registering" were used for the roles in §5, before the definition. Moved to §7.
- Three loose uses of "depend" replaced, since the word now names the bound relation.
