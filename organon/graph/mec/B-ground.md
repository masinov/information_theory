In some domains, the receiving contrast is partially grounded in the target contrast.

## Adds
A domain-specific kind of C.

## Compatible because
Ground relates facts, is asymmetric, holds between what actually obtains, and admits a partial form.

## Closed if
Ground requires the grounded to be nothing over and above its ground, against R being distinct from F; or its necessitation can be stated only over non-actual cases (C-actual).
