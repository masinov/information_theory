A contrast K is registered by a contrast K′ when K′ holds at least partly in virtue of K. K is then the registered contrast, K′ the registering contrast, and the field that bears K′ is said to register K. Always of a particular contrast; an actual relation between contrasts that hold, not a disposition. A contrast registers every contrast it depends on.
