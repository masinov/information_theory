A contrast K borne by F is registered by a contrast K′ when C holds between them: K′ holds at least partly in virtue of K. The field R that bears K′ is then said to register K. Always registration *of* a particular contrast, never of F without qualification; an actual relation, not a disposition. A contrast registers every contrast it holds at least partly in virtue of (Dec-indiscriminate).
