Eight repairs to the wording of Layer 1, none of which adds a commitment: δ is the respect and the contrast is the particular difference (F-respect); roles belong to contrasts first (F-rolesfield); specification leaves the statement of C and the definition of registration (F-specified); P is stated over differences (F-Pterm); "co-present", "available together", "distinguishable" and "the manner in which R is receptive" are removed (F-residues); "partly" reads "at least partly" (F-partly); D is no longer a displayed thesis (F-Dtrivial); the question of a bound on the receiver's contribution is assigned to a later layer (meta/FR-partial).

## Reasons
Content: each repair removes a place where the text said two things that did not fit, recorded in the friction named. Method: the goal of the project is that every assumption be visible; a term used in two senses, or a word carrying time or modality unannounced, hides one. Found in Claude's review of 2026-11-10; the author accepted the correction ("I accept all your corrections and the phases proposed"). The wordings are Claude's.
