The dependence named by "in virtue of" in C is primitive. The core binds it by five stated conditions and does not analyse it further: it relates contrasts that both hold (C-actual); nothing holds in virtue of itself, and it has a direction (C-asym); it may be partial (C-partial); and whether it holds is fixed by no description of what it relates (C-local). It is of no one kind (C-kind).
