C-partial said the dependence is partial, with a remainder supplied by R. Nothing in P requires a remainder, and every argument of the core needs only "at least partly". The stronger claim also meets F-ground: if K′ held wholly in virtue of K, would it be a second contrast?

## Left open
Whether a contrast can hold wholly in virtue of another and still be a second contrast (F-ground). "Partly" also presupposes that what a contrast holds in virtue of can be divided into contributions; that presupposition is now stated here and nowhere argued.
