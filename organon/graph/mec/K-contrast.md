The registering is a contrast, not a single condition. For a registering to be some registering in particular it must be some way in particular (P1), and under Dec-readingA that is a position in an actual contrast. A receiver with one condition only, differing from nothing actual, would register nothing in particular.

## Does not establish
That the receiving field contributes anything to that contrast.

## Origin
Test-cold-1, defect 5: C assumed it.

## Why withdrawn
2026-11-10, by Dec-claims. It does not follow from P1, which concerns what a relation is of, not the registering. Its content is now part of P2 and is postulated.
