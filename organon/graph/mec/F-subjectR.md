The argument for R says that without a differentiation of its own, "nothing in R could distinguish registration from non-registration". Read literally, R does the distinguishing: something like noticing, which E6 excludes as a precondition of registration. Read as the account's distinguishing, the sentence says only that the theorist must be able to pick out something in R, which is a different and weaker claim.

## Origin
Raised by the author: why should R carry any of the work of distinguishing what it registers? The theorist distinguishes; R's differentiation matters only insofar as R is itself part of the field.

## Resolution (2026-11-09)
The literal reading is given up: R distinguishes nothing. Arg-R is restated without the word.
