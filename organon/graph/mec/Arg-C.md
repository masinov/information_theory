That F bears a contrast and that another field bears one leave the two contrasts unconnected. Matching is symmetric (resemblance, correspondence). By P, the distinguishing must depend on what is distinguished; so registration needs an asymmetric dependence between the contrasts, which no description of the relata supplies.
