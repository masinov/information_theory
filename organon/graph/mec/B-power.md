C is the exercise of a receptive capacity of R, prompted by the contrast in F.

## Adds
Capacities as constituents of relata. Settles Ps-modal: "admits" names a capacity. Explains C-partial: R's capacity is the remainder.

## Compatible because
A capacity can be exercised in an actual instance.

## Closed if
A capacity can be identified only by what it would do in non-actual circumstances (E4, C-actual); or no sense can be given to capacities in non-physical domains (C-kind).
