In an instance of C, the contrast depended upon is the *registered* contrast and the contrast that depends is the *registering* contrast. The field that bears the first is the *target* in that instance; the field that bears the second, the *receiver*. Roles belong to contrasts first and to fields through them; one field may be both, through different contrasts. Roles are positions in C, not natures of what occupies them.
