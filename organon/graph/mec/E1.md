The reconstruction concerns a respect whose holding depends, even partly, on the specification or its notation (order, labels and grouping of terms are examples), where the bearer of that respect is not the target; this includes respects relating target positions to notation positions.

## Excluded by
D-scope, as a matter of scope, not as a result of the core.

## Revision (2026-10-25)
By Dec-groundD, the former E1 is split into three exclusions with separate sources: this one (from the description), E1-self and E1-recv (from the receiver). Previous wording, excluded by D and P: "All differentiation is supplied by the receiver (pure constructivism)." The label was Claude's; nothing in the author's draft takes a position on a school of thought.

## Mixed differentiations (2026-11-09)
"Even partly" is kept (the author, 2026-11-09). It follows from "bears": if δ* is "δ, and a is listed first", F bears δ and does not bear δ*, since part of what makes δ* hold is not in F. A mixed differentiation is not excluded wholesale: the contrast F bears is within scope. This agrees with the draft's sentence, which concerns differentiations "supplied entirely by an unrelated description".
