A fresh agent with no project context read only the paper's source, restated it, and listed its defects. Run under the author's delegation of 2026-11-10; the protocol asks for the author's agreement because it costs a separate agent.

## Restatement
Its list of what the paper assumes matched the register in every item and kind. Two sentences it could not place: whether "a term's being some way in a respect is its position among actual differences" is a definition, a consequence or a metaphysical claim; and whether (C) is asserted or is a schema.

## Defects it found, and what was done
1. The argument for P2 rejected likeness because it makes K′ no more of K than of other contrasts, while the paper concedes registration is indiscriminate. Accepted: F-P2circular.
2. The argument for P2 presupposed that what K′ is of is what accounts for K′, which is P2. Accepted: P2 is now a postulate (Dec-Pstatus, revised).
3. The necessity claim covered (R) and the asymmetry, which rested on a scope condition; and the analogy between a contrast made partly by a description and one made partly by its registration fails, since the second is still borne by F. Accepted: Dec-direction.
4. "Conditions without which nothing could be registered" against "the core does not provide for registering which way"; and the answer to the objection from intrinsic character inferred from what can be specified to what can be registered. Accepted: claims narrowed, the answer reworded over determinacy, not specification.
5. That the registering is a contrast was assumed, not argued. Accepted: K-contrast.
6. With the unit-set case, every contrast is registered, against "an articulation may be realized while no contrast holds in virtue of it". Accepted: F-ubiquity.
7. The constitutive case named no respect. Accepted: the case is restated with collections and a named respect.
8. "Checked without appeal to alternatives" was unsupported for the causal case; and conditions at different times are both "actual" only on an unstated assumption about time. Accepted: F-evidence, F-tense.
9. Transitivity open leaves undetermined whether a registering reaches beyond its immediate ground; registering that two terms do not differ has no K. Accepted: stated among the limitations; F-sameness.
10. The dichotomy in P1 was assumed exhaustive; the stated falsifier of P is unusable. Accepted: the exhaustiveness is argued and the one unargued step flagged; the paper now says no case can refute the postulate.
11. Terms used and not listed (respect, partly, "form", attributable to, the modal words of the exposition). Accepted: defined, removed, or listed as presupposed.
12. Minor wording (spatial idiom, "commitment" in two senses). Corrected.
