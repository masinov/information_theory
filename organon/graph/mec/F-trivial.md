Characterized only as asymmetric, actual and partial, the primitive dependence may say too little: many relations have these properties. If no instance can be shown to differ from correspondence by C alone (and C-local says no description fixes it), C-prim risks being a name for the difference rather than an account of it.

## Schema reading (2026-11-10)
By Dec-schema the core is a schema and each domain supplies the dependence. This friction stays open until at least two realizations are worked, one of them not physical. It bears equally on "bears a contrast" (F-bears).

## Resolution (2026-11-10)
Test-realizations. The core does not by itself tell registration from correspondence, and is not meant to: it is a schema, and in each realization the dependence is identified by the means of its own domain. Cost accepted and stated in the paper: the schema is permissive and does not mark off the epistemic.
