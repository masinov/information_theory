If one contrast obtains partly in virtue of another, the second does not obtain, even partly, in virtue of the first. The asymmetry holds between contrasts, not between fields: F and R may each depend on the other through different contrasts. Whether "partly in virtue of" is transitive is left open.

## Consequences
Nothing holds, even partly, in virtue of itself. A registered contrast does not hold, even partly, in virtue of the contrast that registers it (E1-recv). Not excluded: a contrast K′ holds partly in virtue of K, and a further contrast K″ of the first field holds partly in virtue of K′. With transitivity open, a loop through a third contrast is not excluded either.
