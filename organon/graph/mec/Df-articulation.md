What an account uses to speak of a particular contrast: ⟨a,δ,b⟩_F names two terms, a and b, and a respect, δ. The contrast of a realized articulation is the difference of a and b in δ; two articulations may share a respect and have different contrasts. The schema does not assume that a and b exist prior to the contrast as independent objects, and does not deny it.
