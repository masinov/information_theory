Whether K′ holds in virtue of K need not be fixed by a description of the two contrasts. In some kinds of case it is (the constitutive case of Test-realizations); in others it is not: two pairs of contrasts alike in every description, and in any pairing of their terms, may differ in whether the second holds in virtue of the first.
