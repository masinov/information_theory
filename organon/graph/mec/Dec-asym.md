C-asym reads "not even partly": two contrasts do not each hold partly in virtue of the other. Whether "partly in virtue of" is transitive is left open.

## Reasons
Claude recommended both; the author decided. Content: Df-roles defines target and receiver by which contrast depends on which; if K and K′ each held partly in virtue of the other, neither role would be defined for that pair. Method: P does not require the strict reading (P says the distinguishing must depend on X, not that X must not depend on the distinguishing), so part of the basis is the definition of roles and Mp-parsimony. Transitivity is left open for the same economy with which §7 of the paper leaves open whether order is transitive: asserting it would add a partial order among contrasts that nothing yet needs.

## Cost
Excluded: that a registered contrast is partly made by its own registration. E1-recv excludes only direct dependence back; a loop through a third contrast is not excluded while transitivity is open.

## Reasons replaced (2026-11-10)
By Dec-asymscope: the strict reading is grounded in the scope condition, not in the definition of roles. The decision itself stands.

## Revised (2026-11-10)
By Dec-direction: the strict reading is dropped; irreflexivity and direction are kept; mutual dependence is left open.
