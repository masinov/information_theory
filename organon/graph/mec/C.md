A contrast K′, borne by a field, holds partly in virtue of the contrast K of a specified articulation realized in F.

## Does not establish
Which way F is; understanding, representation, order, acquisition, retention.

## Revision (2026-11-09)
By Dec-fieldaspect. C relates two contrasts and no longer mentions R, conditions of R, or matching: R is whatever bears K′ (see R), and the term-by-term pairing was not used by the argument for C (F-pairing). The wording is Claude's, approved by the author before reading it in the paper. Previous wording, requiring R, Df-articulation and Df-conditions: "Through C, the contrast of a specified articulation realized in F is matched by a contrast between conditions of R, and the latter contrast obtains partly in virtue of the former."
