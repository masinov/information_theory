Something of F appears only where another contrast holds, at least partly, in virtue of a contrast of F.

## Does not establish
That anything is taken up; which contrast, among those depended on, a taking-up is of.

## Revision (2026-11-09)
By Dec-fieldaspect. C relates two contrasts and no longer mentions R, conditions of R, or matching: R is whatever bears K′ (see R), and the term-by-term pairing was not used by the argument for C (F-pairing). The wording is Claude's, approved by the author before reading it in the paper. Previous wording, requiring R, Df-articulation and Df-conditions: "Through C, the contrast of a specified articulation realized in F is matched by a contrast between conditions of R, and the latter contrast obtains partly in virtue of the former."

## Revision (2026-11-10)
By Dec-repairs (F-specified, F-partly): specification is no longer part of C, and "partly" reads "at least partly". An account states an instance by specifying an articulation whose contrast is K. Previous wording: "A contrast K′, borne by a field, holds partly in virtue of the contrast K of a specified articulation realized in F."

## Revision (2026-11-10, rebuild after the close reading)
By Dec-rebuild. C is a commitment about a taking-up, in ordinary words; it says which relation the account is about. The relation between two contrasts that the earlier wordings stated is now the definition of registration (Df-registration), reached through As-receiver. Refusal: holding that something can be taken up although nothing holds in virtue of it.
Previous wording: "A contrast K′, borne by a field, holds at least partly in virtue of a contrast K borne by F."

## Revision (2026-11-10, the lean version)
By Dec-lean. Stated directly between two contrasts, as in the author's draft, and for perception only: D holds of whatever is perceived or said, C of what is perceived (Dec-restrictP). "Taken up" is no longer used. Previous wording: "The taking-up holds, at least partly, in virtue of what is taken up."

## Revision (2026-11-10, "appears")
By Dec-appear. The receiving side is no longer described as perceiving. That appearing always has a where is what C states; it is not part of the meaning of "appear". Previous wording: "Something of F is perceived only where a contrast borne by what perceives holds, at least partly, in virtue of a contrast of F."
