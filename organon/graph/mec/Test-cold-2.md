A second fresh agent read only the paper's source, after the revision that followed Test-cold-1. It was asked to judge the paper against its own aim: to say exactly what it assumes and to claim nothing stronger than its support.

## Restatement
Its list of assumptions matched the paper's table. Four sentences whose kind it could not determine: that a basic epistemic relation is to something in particular; the identity of contrasts; "a term's being some way in a respect is its position among the actual differences"; and what comparison requires.

## Defects it found, and what was done
1. "The registering is a contrast" did not follow from P1, and C was stronger than P2 while claiming to add nothing. Accepted: it is now part of P2, a postulate; K-contrast is withdrawn as a consequence.
2. P1's argument showed that what is registered differs, not that the difference is what is registered. Accepted: a second flagged step.
3. "And not conversely", and reasons from symmetry, survived after mutual dependence was left open. Removed.
4. "In virtue of" was used of things that are not contrasts, against its own first condition. Accepted: the conditions bind it as it figures in C; elsewhere "due to" or "because" is the ordinary word.
5. The constitutive case used collections formed by the account, which the scope condition puts outside the reconstruction. Accepted: replaced by a case with no grouping (a difference in a coarser respect due to a difference in a finer one).
6. "Registered" had two senses, the premise's and the defined one. Accepted: the premise now speaks of what a basic epistemic relation is of; "registration" is only the defined term; the paper says the converse of the premise is not claimed.
7. Claims stronger than their support: "nothing could be registered", "excludes the view that a repertoire… order… a subject", "contains no order". Accepted: Dec-claims.
8. D did not follow where a contrast has more than two positions; "and indeed from the definition of a field" was false. Accepted: a contrast has two positions; the clause is deleted.
9. The table omitted or misplaced items. Accepted: rows added for what is assumed outright and what is stipulated; the costs of the decision moved to its row; specification-independence tagged once, as the condition of method; the conditions on "bears" are four.
10. Minor: a non sequitur for irreflexivity (now stipulated); "articulation" defined as a differentiation yet able to pick out nothing; the title "Second field"; the requirements of comparison, retention and acquisition asserted, not derived (now said to be so).

## Not run
A third reader. Each of the two found defects the other had not; a third would probably find more.
