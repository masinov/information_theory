Field-aspect is adopted, with its consequences: C relates two contrasts and no longer mentions matching; R is a consequence of C, C-asym and Field-aspect; the term-by-term pairing is not part of the core; the argument for R no longer has R distinguish.

## Reasons
Content: the argument for R made R distinguish, against E6 (F-subjectR); the pairing of a with r and b with r′ was used by no argument (F-pairing); being differentiated belongs to the concept of field, not to receivers (the author's point, Field-aspect). Method: Mp-parsimony; one commitment (C) and two primitives ("bears a contrast", "partly in virtue of") in place of three commitments.

## Provenance
Field-aspect is the author's. The new wordings of C, R, Arg-R, Df-field, Df-conditions and Df-registration are Claude's. The author approved them on 2026-11-09 ("Go with your suggestions. I'll read it and critique afterwards"), so they are open to the author's critique of the revised paper.

## Cost
"Matched" leaves C, the paper's central thesis. The core now rests on two unanalysed primitives, and F-trivial bears on both. Pairing, where needed (Which-position), must enter as a commitment stated as such.
