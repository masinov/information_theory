A receiver merely differing within itself, where its contrast does not hold even partly in virtue of the target's contrast, is registration.

## Excluded by
C ("partly in virtue of"). A field may register one of its own contrasts, but only through a distinct contrast of its own: C-asym implies that nothing holds in virtue of itself.
