What differs from no actual other term in a given respect is a term of no contrast in that respect, and the core provides for taking up nothing of it in that respect.

## Does not establish
That there is any such thing.

## Origin
Report of the ground-of-D exercise, §7; stated by Dec-repairs.

## Revision (2026-11-10, after the second cold reader)
"In a given respect" throughout; "provides for registering nothing" in place of "nothing can be registered", since the core does not say what cannot occur (Dec-claims).
