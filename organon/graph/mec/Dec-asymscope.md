The strict reading of C-asym ("not even partly") is a matter of scope, not a result. The reconstruction concerns a differentiation attributable to its target (D-scope): a contrast made in part by a description lies outside it (E1), and a contrast made in part by its own registration lies outside it for the same reason (E1-recv).

## Reasons
Content: Dec-asym grounded the strict reading in the definition of roles, which is a weak ground: one could define roles otherwise. The route condition of the author's draft gives one ground for both guards. Method: P does not require the strict reading, so presenting it as a result claimed more than was supported. Claude proposed it and asked whether it is faithful to what the author meant by the route condition; the author accepted. Dec-asym's decision stands; its reasons are replaced by these.
