A contrast registers every contrast it holds at least partly in virtue of. The core does not single out one of them. Narrowing to one is not a condition of registration; a later layer must supply the means.

## Reasons
Content: once specification is removed from the definition of registration (F-specified), nothing in C selects among the contrasts a registering contrast depends on, and if the dependence is transitive they include whatever the registered contrast itself depends on. Method: the core claims necessary conditions only (Q0); selection is a further achievement, to be added as a commitment stated as such. Found in Claude's review of 2026-11-10; the author accepted the correction ("I accept all your corrections and the phases proposed"). Claude called it the largest concession of the review.
