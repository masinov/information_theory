Enough individuation to distinguish F from R and to take r, r′ as conditions of one relatum; no more.

## Examined (2026-11-10)
Dec-primitive. Two contrasts are the same when they have the same positions and the same respect; no other individuation is assumed. F and R are not individuated beyond being what bears the contrasts concerned (Field-aspect).
