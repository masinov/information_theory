The paper is written again, at about half its length. D is in the author's form, with the something first. C is stated directly between two contrasts and for perception only. "Taken up" is dropped; "perceived" is used, in the widest sense. If F is everything, it is identified through the differences within it (mec/Totality). Each thing is said once: the paper no longer reports its own bookkeeping, lists ways of refusing its commitments, or lists what it leaves undone.

## Reasons
In the author's words. On the form: "there is a huge amount of redundance, an orthopedic articulation of what is being said, repeating over and over again, almost apologizing constantly for what is or is not said"; of the abstract: "extremely verbose and again, self apologizing". On D: "I do not understand why suddenly you decided to make taking up an differentiation the core… 'something in particular'... out of where? and it is taken up as it differs from 'something else'... what? Does this not pressure you to speak about the thing being differentiated first as the fundamental object instead of juggling around it?"

## What is kept from Dec-rebuild
The decision for actual terms as a decision with its reasons; no "position"; the conditions on the two notions; that registration is necessary for perception and is not itself epistemic; the receiving side as resting on the decision (one sentence in §5: "A receiver with a single condition is outside the account").

## What is no longer in the paper, and stays in organon
The ways of refusing each commitment; the paragraph on what kind of claim is made and on Stroud's objection; the list of eight things left undone; the two unargued assumptions about the receiving side as a separate passage (mec/As-receiver); the scope condition as a displayed thesis (the author's sentence is kept in §2).
