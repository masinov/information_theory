Registration, or at least the acquisition and retention built on it, requires time.

## Why withdrawn
Raised in the review of the first draft. The author objected that time is a loaded notion and that claiming it necessary overreaches: structures that are ordered but not temporal may suffice. The core keeps only an asymmetric relation, with transitivity left open (E5, Q4). See F-time.
