D is no longer admitted as a commitment. It splits into a consequence of the definitions (D: every field in which a registered articulation is realized bears that articulation's contrast) and a condition of method (D-scope: the reconstruction articulates only fields taken to bear the contrasts it specifies). The draft's guard against differences supplied by a description is D-scope; the guard against the receiver belongs to C and C-asym. E1 splits into three exclusions, each with its own source, and loses the label "pure constructivism".

## Reasons
The author chose S1 of the six-round exercise (notebook/2026-10-24-ground-of-D/report.md), Claude's recommendation. Content: the paper's single ground of D mixed two guards with different sources; the receiver guard follows from C, and D as stated did two jobs that come apart. Method: the description guard concerns the account's relation to its targets, which is aboutness by description, outside the core's scope under Dec-restrictP; the author's draft already calls it "a necessary condition for that route of explanation".

## Included in the choice
- D is not modal. The draft's sentence "Differentiability is consequently a modal commitment" is to be revised. A modal D would need non-actual terms, against Dec-readingA, or a capacity of F (B-power).
- E1 excludes respects that depend even partly on the specification, which is stronger than the draft's "supplied entirely".
- "F bears a contrast in respect δ" is primitive (Df-bears).
The author may revisit any of these separately.

## Not decided
- Whether Field-aspect's "subject to differentiation" means "can be differentiated" or "bears a contrast".
- Whether "partly in virtue of" is transitive, and whether C-asym reads "not even partly"; E1-recv relies on the second.
- The further points in §7 of the report.

## Consequences
C no longer requires D: D now follows from registration, which presupposes C. R and Df-articulation require D-scope and Df-bears instead. Ps-modal, as a presupposition of D, lapses; it stays open for R until Field-aspect is decided.
