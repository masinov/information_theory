Contrasts hold only between actual, co-present terms; the core admits no repertoire of alternatives (Reading A), rather than contrasts between an actual term and its alternatives (Reading B).

## Reasons
In the author's words: "Must be A. B needs some sort of alternative repository or other metaphysical assumptions we do not want to make." The reason is partly about content (an alternative must be held somewhere, which the core does not supply) and partly methodological (Mp-parsimony). Recorded under M4 as resting on both.

## Consequences
Ps-actual, E4; all material on alternatives (Shannon, Dretske's probabilities, relevant alternatives, contrastivism, counterfactual and interventionist analyses) is confined to one paragraph of §6 on what the core does not include.
