What must hold for knowledge of any kind to be possible, prior to knowledge itself? Layer 1 (the paper) gives the necessary conditions D, R and C; Layer 2 examines their presuppositions.

## Frame
The home frame of the core is a frame like any other, not a neutral default. It uses the general method in organon/method, with no overrides. What it holds fixed while the rest is examined (holds_fixed): the premise P; Reading A and the actuality of contrasts that goes with it; the use of logic in exposition (Ps-logic); and the methodological presuppositions of parsimony, classical logic and foundational building. Under Mp-separable, any of these can become content and be revised.

## Builds on
Nothing. Later investigations (order and retention; the epistemic apparatus) are to build on this one.
