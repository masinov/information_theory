The paper was rewritten once, as a whole, from the notes of the close reading (notebook/2026-11-10-close-reading.md; the changes are tied to the notes in notebook/2026-11-10-revision-log.md). Nine things were decided.

1. **What the core is.** A structure that every taking-up contains, not a kind of epistemic relation. "Basic form" is no longer used. Every taking-up contains a registration; a registration is not, as such, a taking-up.
2. **Two commitments.** (D) Differentiation: what is taken up is something in particular, and it is taken up as it differs, in some respect, from something else. (C) Reception: the taking-up holds, at least partly, in virtue of what is taken up. P is withdrawn as a separate premise, and Pr-det and Pr-rec as separate principles; their content is in D and C.
3. **Actual terms only is the author's decision** (Dec-readingA), not a consequence. Three reasons, each in the form that holds (Arg-actual). The first is the author's own: an alternative has to be supplied from somewhere, and the three sources are a stock of possibilities, a repertoire, or the account's own specification, which D-scope excludes.
4. **The receiving side** is treated by the decision, with two assumptions that are not argued (As-receiver). Nothing in D and C requires that what receives have two actual conditions.
5. **"Position" is dropped.** A contrast is between two terms, which are what differ. An articulation names two terms and a respect. Whether terms exist prior to their contrasts is left open.
6. **Three conditions on each unanalysed notion.** "Bears a contrast": in a respect; two terms, none differing from itself; both actual. "In virtue of": relates contrasts that both hold; nothing holds in virtue of itself; it has a direction. That the dependence may be partial, mutual or transitive, and whether it is fixed by description, are not conditions. Transitivity and mutual dependence exclude each other.
7. **The scope condition** concerns contrasts that hold because the account has specified them, and nothing else. A contrast of a field may hold partly in virtue of a contrast borne elsewhere.
8. **(C) is a commitment about a taking-up**, stated in §2. Registration is defined once, in §7, and the paper says by which steps every taking-up contains one.
9. **The second case is given in letters** (a finer and a coarser respect).

## Reasons
Content: each point answers numbered notes of the close reading; the log gives the correspondence. Method: the paper had been patched about ten times in one day and most conflicts lay at the joints, so it was rewritten in one piece and then read through once more before anything was recorded. The count of what the account rests on is again close to the author's draft: a differentiated target (D), a differentiated receiving side (now the decision applied to it), and receptive dependence (C).

## What this reverses
Dec-principles (that P follows from two principles and that actual terms only follows from receptivity). Parts of Dec-primitive (the counts of conditions) and of Test-audit-1 (its corrections 4 and 6 are superseded).

Taken by Claude under the author's delegation of 2026-11-10; open to the author's critique.

## Revised in form (2026-11-10)
By Dec-lean. Points 3 to 9 stand. Points 1 and 2 are restated: D is in the author's form and "taking-up" is no longer used.
