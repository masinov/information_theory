Every field in which a registered articulation is realized bears that articulation's contrast.

## Does not establish
Which contrasts a field bears, or of what form; that any field bears a contrast apart from registration; any modal claim about F. What the reconstruction may articulate is D-scope, a condition of method.

## Revision (2026-10-25)
By Dec-groundD, D is a consequence of Df-registration and Df-realized (Arg-D), no longer an admitted commitment, and is not modal. Previous wording, as a commitment requiring P: "F admits differentiation, although no particular differentiation is thereby realized or specified."
