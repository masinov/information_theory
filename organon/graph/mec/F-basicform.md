The paper defined the basic form of an epistemic relation as a kind of relation (perception, prior to concepts), and elsewhere as a structure that is not epistemic by itself. The title, the premise and the main result all depended on the phrase.

## Resolution
The second sense. The core is what every taking-up contains; "basic form" is not used.
