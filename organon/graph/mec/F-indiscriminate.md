If K′ holds partly in virtue of K and partly in virtue of another contrast K*, it stands in C to both; if the dependence is transitive, also to whatever K depends on. Only the account's specification singled out which contrast "is" registered, and specification is not part of registration (F-specified).

## Cost accepted
Registration at this level is of every contrast depended upon. Aboutness of one thing rather than another is not delivered by the core.
