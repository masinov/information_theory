Registration of which way F is may need no non-actual alternatives, if the alternatives are actual conditions of R. A receiver can have an actual, co-present differentiation into many conditions (as organon has a list of statuses); registering which way F is would be: which of R's actual conditions answers, through C, to the term realized in F. The other conditions are actual too, but nothing in F answers to them.

## Open
Whether this says which way F is, or only which way R is: the unoccupied conditions are actual in R, but their relevance to F seems to be exactly what alternatives supplied. If that relevance can be stated without modality, F-which closes within Reading A.
