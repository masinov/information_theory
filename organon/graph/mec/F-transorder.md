If "in virtue of" is transitive and has no loops, dependence already orders contrasts, and is a candidate for the loose order Q4 asks for. If it is not, loops through a third contrast are not excluded and "nothing holds in virtue of itself" holds only directly. Layer 1 leaves it open (Dec-asym); it cannot be decided apart from Q4.
