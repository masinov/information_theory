The reconstruction articulates only fields taken to bear the contrasts it specifies, and concerns only contrasts the account specifies. Specification chooses the respect and selects a contrast borne in it; that it does not create the contrast is a stipulation of the route, not a result.

## Source
The author's draft, §1: differentiability is admitted "because the proposed reconstruction concerns a differentiation attributable to its target, rather than one supplied entirely by an unrelated description. It is a necessary condition for that route of explanation." Stated as method because the account's relation to its targets is aboutness by description, outside P (Dec-restrictP).

## What it does not do
It does not argue against the view that all differentiation is supplied. It states that view's cost: if it held, nothing would be registered in P's sense.

## Heuristic
Relabelling the terms of an articulation, or replacing ⟨a,δ,b⟩ by ⟨b,δ⁻¹,a⟩, where δ⁻¹ is the converse respect, must not change whether the articulation is realized. A heuristic only: as a definition of "merely notational" it is circular, since positions are individuated by the contrasts under test.

## Extended to registration (2026-11-10)
By Dec-asymscope. The same condition grounds the strict asymmetry of C: a differentiation attributable to its target is supplied, even in part, neither by a description (E1) nor by its own registration (E1-recv). The statement above is unchanged.
