After Test-cold-2. (1) That the registering is a difference is part of P2, a postulate, not a consequence. (2) P1 is argued up to two flagged steps. (3) "Registration" is only the defined term; the premise says every basic epistemic relation is a registration and does not claim the converse. (4) Of the former exclusions, only E2 is ruled out (resemblance and correspondence do not supply the dependence P2 requires); E1 is a matter of scope; E1-self says what is not a registration of anything in the target; E3, E4, E5 and E6 are read as "not among the conditions the premise requires", which does not show that registration can occur without them in a given domain. (5) A contrast has two positions. (6) "In virtue of" is bound by its conditions only as it figures in C. (7) Specification-independence is tagged once, as the condition of method.

## Reasons
Content: each point is a place where the paper claimed more than it had shown or tagged an item in two ways (Test-cold-2). Method: the aim is to claim nothing stronger than the support (Q0, "avoid overclaiming necessity"). Taken by Claude under the author's delegation of 2026-11-10; open to the author's critique.
