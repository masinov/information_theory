"Field F bears a contrast in respect δ" is primitive. It is bound by four conditions: a contrast is always in some respect; it has two positions, and no position differs from itself; its positions are both actual; and it does not divide what bears it exhaustively. A contrast is always borne by something. A *position* is a place in a contrast a field bears. A *specified term* is what a description picks out; it may pick out nothing. By stipulation, two contrasts are the same when they have the same positions and the same respect.

## Notes
The two levels keep realization from holding by definition: if a term were simply a position, every articulation would be realized. "Bears" carries the whole weight of the description guard (D-scope) and is not further analysed.
