"Field F bears a contrast in respect δ" is primitive. A *position* is a place in a contrast a field bears. A *specified term* is what a description picks out; it may pick out nothing.

## Notes
The two levels keep realization from holding by definition: if a term were simply a position, every articulation would be realized. "Bears" carries the whole weight of the description guard (D-scope) and is not further analysed.
