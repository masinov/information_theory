A receiver might depend directly on what a term a is in itself, with no contrast involved. Then the contrast K′ in the receiver and the contrast K between a and b would share a ground (a's character) without K′ holding in virtue of K, and C would fail of a case that looks like registration.

## Answer
Under Dec-readingA a term's being some way in a respect is its position among actual differences in that respect. A character apart from every actual contrast could be specified only against alternatives ("this way rather than otherwise", where nothing actual is otherwise), so it lies with the alternatives outside the core. The case is one of registering which way a term is (Q3), which the core leaves open; it is not a case the conditions are meant to cover and fail to.

## Cost
C is necessary for registration within Dec-readingA. Whether terms have such characters is left open (Ps-relata).

## Reworded (2026-11-10)
The answer must not pass from what can be specified to what can be registered (Test-cold-1, defect 4). Stated over determinacy: a character that contrasts with nothing actual would be a way in particular only against alternatives. The core does not say such a character cannot be registered; it says that registering it needs what the core does not contain.
