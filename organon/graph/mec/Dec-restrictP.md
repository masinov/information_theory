P concerns basic aboutness, prior to concepts and descriptions: what is perceived or registered in any way. Aboutness by description, of the future, and of mathematical objects is not covered by P; whether it is built from basic aboutness is the open question Q5.

## Reasons
Content: some aboutness seems to lack the dependence P requires (F-descriptive), so P as first stated claimed too much. Method: restricting keeps the core minimal and moves a disputed thesis, that all knowledge rests on basic aboutness, to the layer where it can be argued (Mp-parsimony). Claude recommended it; the author decided.

## Consequences
The core's necessity (Necessity) holds for basic aboutness. P was held fixed by the core's home frame; it has now been revised by content, the case Mp-separable describes. Its previous wording is kept in P.
