C relates two contrasts, and by C-asym no contrast holds in virtue of itself, so the contrast that depends is a second contrast. Whatever bears a contrast is, insofar as it does, a field (Field-aspect). The receiving field distinguishes nothing: the account picks out its contrast, as it picks out the target's.
