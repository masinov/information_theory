A reason, not a proof. For a thing to be some way in particular there must be a way that it is not, and its being this way as against another lies in that difference. What is not one way as against any other is no way in particular, and cannot be what a taking-up is of. A taking-up of something as the particular thing it is, is then a taking-up of it as it differs. It supposes that to take up something in particular is to take it up in what makes it particular.
