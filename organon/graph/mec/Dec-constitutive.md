(D) reads: "What appears of F is nothing over and above contrasts that F bears." The qualifier "in the elementary case" is removed. The elementary case is one contrast registered. Anything more that appears is contrasts in some organization, with nothing further appearing besides. That the organization the core supplies (dependence: one condition holding in virtue of several contrasts; a condition entering a contrast that is registered in turn) is enough for likeness, for which way a term is, and for a lone term, is a hypothesis and is marked as one in the paper.

## How it was reached
Claude had put two positions to the author: elementary appearances are contrasts while other things may appear through them; or nothing but contrasts ever appears. The author developed the second with the outside referee (the author's own message was not seen by Claude; the referee reports it as the suggestion that a thing and its complete pattern of differences may be two ways of describing what appears, with a monadological motivation). The referee proposed a third formulation, the constitutive one, which the author adopted ("Yes, go").

## What it does not say
- Nothing about what a thing is. Whether a thing has a nature beyond its differences is left open (Ps-relata); the claim is that no such nature appears apart from them. This is the line the author drew on 2026-11-10 against reducing "being some way" to differing, now applied to appearing only.
- Not that a thing is recoverable from its differences. The referee's example: (2, 5, 11) and (12, 15, 21) have the same internal differences and are distinct. Claude's reading, with which the author agreed: what the differences leave undetermined there, the common shift, is what cannot appear without a further term; this is the totality case (mec/Totality). The claim made is: what enters no contrast does not appear.
- Not that every complex appearing is itself one contrast between two terms.

## Open
What "organization" requires beyond the dependence, and whether it can be had without pairing, order or retention. That is the work of the later layers (Q3, Q4).
