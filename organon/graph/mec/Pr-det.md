To be some way in particular is to differ: for there to be a way that the thing is not. Applied without exception: to what a relation is of, to the registering, and to the conditions of whatever receives.

## Notes
Asserted, not derived: it is what the account takes "in particular" to mean. Its ground: a way that excluded nothing would mark nothing, and a thing that was no way in particular could not be what anything is of. It leaves open whether the other term is actual; that is settled by Pr-rec. Point of refusal: holding that a thing may be some way in particular while differing from nothing, actual or not, in that respect.
