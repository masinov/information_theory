The paper left out a notational respect "because part of what makes it hold is not in F". By that reason no contrast that holds partly in virtue of a contrast elsewhere is borne by its field, and no registering contrast is borne by its receiver.

## Resolution
The condition concerns only what holds because the account has specified it.
