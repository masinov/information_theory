Terms are positions within an articulation; whether relations are prior to relata, or the theory is neutral, is not settled.

## Examined (2026-11-10)
Dec-primitive, Obj-intrinsic. For the core, a term's being some way in a respect is its position among actual differences in that respect. Whether terms also have characters apart from every actual contrast is left open: such a character could be specified only against alternatives, so it lies outside the core with them (Dec-readingA).
