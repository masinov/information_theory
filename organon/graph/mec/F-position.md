A position was defined as a place in a contrast, yet the second realization had the same positions in two contrasts, and the word was used in three further senses. The definition was a remainder of the reduction of a thing to its differences, corrected in §2 only.

## Resolution
The word is dropped. A contrast is between two terms, which are what differ.
