In Field-aspect, "subject to differentiation" means "bears a contrast" (Df-bears), not "can be differentiated". Whatever bears a contrast is, insofar as it does, a field.

## Reasons
In the author's words: "I think it is safer, and, while potentialities could technically be inscribed into the system (and may be necessary for knowledge), we can probably introduce them separately and clearly without letting it slip into things accidentally." Content: a modal reading needs non-actual terms, against Dec-readingA, or a capacity of F, which cannot be stated without what it would do (the dilemma in notebook/2026-10-24-ground-of-D/round-6.md). Method: minimality (Mp-parsimony); a potentiality, if needed, is to be added as its own commitment, stated as such.

## Consequences
Field-aspect agrees with D and Dec-groundD: nothing in the core is modal on the side of the fields. Ps-modal is not presupposed by D, and is not presupposed by R once Field-aspect is adopted. Potentialities are not excluded: they are not part of the core, and if they enter, they enter as a separate commitment.
