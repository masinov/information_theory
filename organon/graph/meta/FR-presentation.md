Some fields register differences in the investigation (status, links, kind); others only present it (order, where, and titles: 35 of 110 entries have a title equal to their id, because the schema requires a title where thinking supplied none). Nothing said which is which, so a difference in presentation could be read as a registered difference.

## Resolution
Distinguished: the schema now lists the presentational fields; like the core's conventions, they make no claim about what is recorded.
