Two decisions used `requires` to mean that they concern D, R or C, not that they rest on them. The dependency list of the register then said that Reading A depends on D and R, which is false: D and R were reread in the light of Reading A.

## Resolution
Distinguished: `requires` is reserved for dependence; a decision names what it concerns with `about`. Both decisions changed; rule written into the schema.
