C-partial lets the receiver contribute part of the receiving contrast, and E1 excludes only the extreme case, where the receiver contributes all of it. Between the two the core sets no bound. organon passes the core while its format sometimes decided the difference recorded: the stance vocabulary (accepts, rejects, reinterprets) had no place for "compatible but narrowed", and C-kind was first recorded as not surviving before being corrected. The core cannot tell a recording dominated by its format from a faithful one, as long as the format does not supply everything.

## Resolution (2026-11-10)
Registration in the core is not a matter of degree, so the core sets no bound and claims no remainder (mec/C-partial). How much a receiver may contribute belongs to an account of fidelity, in a later layer. The observation about organon stands as a finding of the reflexive test.
