organon registers a status by selecting one value from a fixed list (open, closed, ...). That is registration against a repertoire of alternatives, the structure E4 excludes as a precondition of registration.

## Resolution
Distinguished. E4 excludes a repertoire as a precondition of registration in general, not its use by a particular receiver, which the exclusions explicitly allow. And the repertoire here is not a stock of possibilities held anywhere: the lists are actual parts of organon, co-present with the value selected. This suggests a route for Q3: mec/Lead-repertoire.
