Every completed occasion is fully definite. "F admits differentiation" becomes: a completed occasion can be taken up by later occasions in more than one way, and how it is taken up is fixed by the later occasion. What makes an occasion definite in the first place is where the frame's branches divide (Br-eternal, Br-actual).

## Lost
D's neutrality. In the core, D realizes no particular differentiation; in the frame every actual occasion is completely determinate.
