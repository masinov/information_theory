The asymmetry of C is the asymmetry between a completed occasion and one in becoming: only what has completed its becoming can be prehended, and contemporaries cannot prehend one another. Between two occasions, dependence never runs both ways. Dependence of each on the other "in different respects" (C-asym) holds only between societies, each of which takes up earlier occasions of the other.

## Lost
The core allows two relata to depend on each other in different respects without restriction; the frame allows it only for relata extended in order (societies), never for single occasions.

## Qualification
Whether distant past occasions are prehended directly or only through intervening ones is disputed in the literature. Nothing here depends on it.
