"Admits" expresses real potentiality: every completed occasion is available to be taken up by any later becoming. The receptive capacity of R (B-power) appears as the subject's own aim, renewed in each occasion rather than held as a standing property. Whether there is in addition a realm of pure potentials is where the branches divide.

## Lost
B-power's capacities are standing properties of enduring things; here the capacity is made anew in each occasion.

## Verification
Whitehead's "principle of relativity" (every being is a potential for every becoming) is stated here from memory; its wording was not confirmed.
