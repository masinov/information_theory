Relations are internal to the occasion that prehends and external to the occasion prehended. The receiver is constituted by its relations; the target is complete before it is related and is not changed by being prehended. This answers Ps-relata asymmetrically: relations are prior to the receiving relatum and posterior to the target.

## Notes
On F-ground: the receiver is partly constituted by what it takes up, as on B-ground, yet it is not nothing over and above it, since its subjective form adds what the datum does not supply. In this frame the tension of F-ground is resolved by that distinction. On Df-articulation: on the target side the terms are completed occasions, prior to the relation; only on the receiving side are they positions within it.
