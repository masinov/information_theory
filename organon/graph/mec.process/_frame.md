The question of the core worked in process philosophy, chiefly Whitehead's. The final real things are occasions: each one comes to be by taking up (prehending) earlier occasions, and is constituted by what it takes up. Relations of taking-up are therefore not added to things already there; they are how the receiving thing comes to be. The frame tests which results of the core survive in a rich scheme built by a different method (rule M5).

## Method
Overrides Mp-parsimony: Whitehead's scheme is deliberately rich. Overrides Mp-foundational: the scheme is judged by the fit of its categories as a whole, not built upward from a minimal trunk. Adds the local presupposition Mp-speculative. M2 (closure by contradiction) stays in force.
