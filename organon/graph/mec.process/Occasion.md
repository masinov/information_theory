R is an occasion in becoming, or a society of occasions: an enduring thing is a series of occasions, each taking up the one before. R is distinct from what it prehends, but not independent of it: it is constituted by its prehensions. Occasions are individuated by their standpoint, that is, by the actual world each one takes up.

## Lost
R as a relatum given before C. In the frame the receiver comes to be in its prehensions.
