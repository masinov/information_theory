A scheme of general ideas is judged by being coherent (its ideas presuppose one another), logical, and, in its interpretation of experience, applicable and adequate. Deduction serves to verify the scheme, not to generate it.

## Cost
There is no minimal trunk: a result is supported by the fit of the whole scheme, so a failure in one place may spread to others.
