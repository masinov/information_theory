Whitehead's own scheme: definiteness comes from eternal objects, pure potentials for the specific determination of fact, which enter (ingress into) occasions. An occasion's definiteness is a selection among them; what is not selected is excluded by negative prehensions.

## Adds
A realm of pure potentials. This is a repertoire of alternatives in the sense of E4, and contrasts may hold between an actual term and a potential one, against Ps-actual.

## Gains
Q3 is answered: which way F is, is which eternal objects have entered it, and F-which closes. Necessary contrasts, as in mathematics, are relations among eternal objects.

## Cost
Exactly what Reading A was adopted to avoid.
