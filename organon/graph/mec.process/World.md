F is the actual world of an occasion: the many earlier occasions available to it. There is no field without a standpoint; F is always the world of some occasion.

## Lost
The core's field implies no presentation to a subject. The frame's world is always relative to an occasion, though not to a knowing subject.

## Note (2026-10-24)
Under mec/Field-aspect, whatever is subject to differentiation is a field insofar as it is. A prehending occasion is itself definite, and so a field in that sense; the frame accepts the proposal.
