Whitehead describes causation as the passing on of feeling from earlier occasions to later ones, while E3 excludes that registration is a transfer from target to receiver.

## Resolution
Distinguished: nothing leaves the earlier occasion, which stays complete and unchanged; the later occasion reproduces a form in its own becoming.
