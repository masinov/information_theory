C is a positive physical prehension: an occasion in becoming takes up an earlier, completed occasion, and the contrast in the receiving occasion holds partly because of the contrast in what it takes up. Every prehension has three factors: the subject that prehends, the datum prehended, and the subjective form, which is how the subject takes the datum. The datum supplies what is taken up; the subjective form is partly the subject's own (C-partial).

## Answer to Q2
Not reduced to anything more elementary: prehension is itself basic in the scheme. But it has an internal structure (three factors), so in this frame C is basic without being bare.

## Lost
The core's neutrality about the kind of C. In the frame every instance of C, physical or not, is prehension, and causation in physical domains (B-cause) is one case of it.
