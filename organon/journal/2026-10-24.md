  `minimal_epistemic_core/frames.md`). Every claim, concept, decision and position of the core has a

- **Result** (test mec.process/Test-robust, result mixed):

    Q1 (the asymmetry of C is completion); F-ground (by a distinction);

    (Br-eternal), Q3 is answered and F-which closes, but Reading A is lost. Without them (Br-actual,

    artefact of the core's method.
