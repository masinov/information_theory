Rule M5 as a procedure.

## Procedure
Choose a step. Rebuild it under a different method: richer instead of minimal, coherentist instead of foundational, a different logic. Compare the results.

## Record
A test entry listing which results survive and which change; results that change are tagged with the methodological presupposition they depend on.
