Apply the content of an investigation to the apparatus used to pursue it, and record where they disagree.

## Procedure
Take the investigation's claims about its own subject matter and ask whether the apparatus (organon, its schema, its tools, the way entries are made and linked) satisfies them, or presupposes their denial. Write predictions first (T-bet). For each disagreement, decide whether the content or the apparatus must change, and record it as a friction between the content entry and the method or schema entry.

## Record
A test entry; frictions for each disagreement; changes to the schema or method are recorded in the journal with the friction that forced them.

## Why
A theory of the preconditions of knowledge must hold of the knowledge it produces. A frame built without regard to the content may impose the denial of what the content asserts.
