Frame and content differ by role, not by kind. At any moment some entries function as frame (they are held fixed while others are examined) and others as content; the same entry can play either role, and content can force a change in what is held fixed. Each opener records what it holds fixed in `holds_fixed`.

## Cost
No entry is protected by being method. A rule or presupposition can be refuted by what an investigation finds, and closures that rested on it (M4) must then be reopened. Conversely, nothing is examined without something else held fixed for the moment.

## Background
Carnap's separation of questions within a framework from the choice of framework; Quine's denial of a sharp line; Davidson's critique of the dualism of scheme and content; Wittgenstein's riverbed, whose bed shifts (On Certainty §§96–99). All cited from memory; not checked.
