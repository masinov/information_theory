Find technical terms used before they are defined, or not defined at all.

## Procedure
List the technical terms of a draft. Each must be defined in the text or replaced by a plain one. Rewrite a central passage in plain words; if that cannot be done, the passage may not be understood yet. Example: "the contrast obtains in virtue of" means "the contrast holds because of".

## Record
Changes to the draft; a test entry only if a term proves to hide a real unclarity.
