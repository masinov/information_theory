For a claim of necessity: does the argument show that it holds of what is described, or only of how things must be, or seem, for a receiver of some kind?

## Procedure
State which reading the argument supports, and what would be needed for the stronger one.

## Record
A test entry linked to the claim and to meta/Q-necessity.
