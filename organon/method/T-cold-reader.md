A reader with no history in the project states what a draft claims.

## Procedure
Give a draft to a fresh agent with no project context, and ask it to list the claims and the arguments for them. Compare with the register. A difference means the text is unclear or that we have come to take something for granted. Run only with the author's agreement, since it costs a separate agent.

## Record
A test entry with the reader's list and the differences found.
