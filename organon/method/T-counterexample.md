Look for a case that satisfies the premises of a claim but not its conclusion.

## Procedure
State the claim and its premises. Construct candidate cases, varying one feature at a time. Include domains that are not physical and not temporal, since the investigations claim conditions "of any kind". For each candidate, say which premises it satisfies and whether the conclusion holds.

## Record
A test entry listing the candidates and the result; any surviving counterexample becomes an objection entry.
