# The core in other frames

A frame works the question of the core (Q0) in another system of thought, to see which results
survive (rule M5). For each frame: its stance on every entry of the core, its own entries, its
frictions, and the result of the robustness test. Ids without a prefix are entries of the core; the
frame's own entries are in `organon/graph/<frame>/`.

The core's own namespace is a frame too, not a neutral default (`Mp-separable`): the first table
sets it beside the others. "Holds fixed" lists what each frame keeps out of examination for the
moment; under `Mp-separable` any of it can become content and be revised.
